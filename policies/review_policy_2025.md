# Meeting Minutes Review Policy (Version 2)

- **Effective date:** 2025-03-01
- **Status:** DEPRECATED — superseded by Version 3.2 (effective
  2026-10-01). Do **not** use this policy to classify minutes; it is kept in
  this folder for archive purposes only.

## Review Level assignment (old)

1. **Executive Review** — the minute records 20 or more attendees.
2. **Team Review** — the minute records at least 10 attendees and
   2 or more action items.
3. **Standard Review** — every other minute.

## Sub-type exclusion (old)

Minutes of type `weekly-sync` were always classified as Standard Review under
this version.

## Follow-up pool (old)

Under this version a minute entered the follow-up pool when it had
2 or more action items.
